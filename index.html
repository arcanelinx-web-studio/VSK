One company.
Several engineering disciplines.

Instead of displaying every service as a separate card, this concept lets the visitor open only the capability they care about and keep the machine/project evidence beside it.

Start from the operation, not from a standard machine.

Application study, machine concept, mechanical design, hydraulic and pneumatic systems, control architecture, fabrication, assembly, trials and commissioning can be developed as one project.

  • Turning, boring, cutting and drilling
  • Testing and inspection equipment
  • Handling and loading systems
  • Process and finishing equipment

Mechanical restoration and controls modernization belong together.

VSK’s retrofit work combines machine correction with electrical rebuilding, CNC/PLC/HMI modernization, servo and drive integration, panel work, wiring and recommissioning.

  • Grinding and jig-grinding references
  • CNC / PLC / HMI upgrades
  • Servo and drive modernization
  • Panel, wiring and recommissioning

Process thinking continues into the component.

Turning, machining and grinding knowledge is combined with workholding, fixture and process support to help create repeatable production from customer drawings and requirements.

  • Precision components to drawing
  • Turning, machining and grinding
  • Jigs, fixtures and interfaces
  • Manufacturability support

The control system is part of the machine architecture.

Controls engineering includes CNC/PLC/HMI integration, servo and VFD systems, panel design, wiring, machine electrification and accessory integration across new and existing equipment.

  • CNC / PLC / HMI integration
  • Servo and VFD motion systems
  • Electrical panels and wiring
  • Accessory and peripheral integration

# VSK Electro-Mech Solutions — Website Design Options

This file is for client review before any hosting/domain/deployment decision.

## Option A — Engineered / Editorial
Branch: `main`

**Positioning:** Integrated engineering partner.

**Visual language:** Dark technical hero, VSK octagonal geometry, machine-DNA visualization, interactive project archive, engineering-flow graphics, real-machine motion, strong proof metrics and structured RFQ.

**Best for:** A client who wants the website to communicate breadth of engineering capability and look modern/technical without becoming overly cinematic.

**Primary impression:** `Engineering depth + integrated capability + modern industrial brand`

---

## Option B — Cinematic Machine Showcase
Branch: `option-b-cinematic`

**Positioning:** The machines are the brand.

**Visual language:** Full-screen real-machine footage, dramatic typography, sticky image/video chapters for BUILD / REVIVE / MAKE, large-format project photography, minimal UI chrome and cinematic transitions.

**Best for:** A client who wants a memorable, premium site that feels closer to a global machine-tool campaign or exhibition experience.

**Primary impression:** `Premium + visual + bold + machine-first`

---

## Option C — Precision / European Industrial
Branch: `option-c-precision`

**Positioning:** Precision engineering organization.

**Visual language:** Light background, strict grid, precise typography, technical register rows, specification-style project presentation, restrained motion, thin-line detailing and measured blue accents.

**Best for:** A client who wants a highly professional, clean, European/German/Swiss industrial feel with less visual drama and more engineering discipline.

**Primary impression:** `Precision + trust + sophistication + corporate engineering`

---

## What stays consistent in all three

- Exact VSK logo and two-blue brand identity
- Real VSK machine/project photography
- Verified VSK service/capability content
- Machine-building, retrofit/reconditioning and precision-manufacturing positioning
- Documented project proof points such as 20 µm, 0.02 mm and 12 sec where used
- Google review/social-proof direction
- Direct phone, email and WhatsApp enquiry path
- Responsive design and reduced-motion support
- No stock photography required

## Client decision question

**Which direction best represents how you want VSK Electro-Mech Solutions to be perceived?**

- **A — Engineered / Editorial**
- **B — Cinematic Machine Showcase**
- **C — Precision / European Industrial**

The selected direction will then be refined into the final site. Content, section order, imagery and animation can still be adjusted after the visual direction is chosen.
